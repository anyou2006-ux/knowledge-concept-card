# English Vocabulary Profile

Use for one English word, collocation, idiom, phrasal verb, or short phrase.

## Required Content

- headword or phrase, including capitalization where relevant
- IPA: specify UK and US when they differ materially
- part of speech and inflection information when useful
- concise Chinese definition, ordered by frequency and learner relevance
- learner-friendly English definition, not a circular dictionary paraphrase
- one natural example sentence and faithful Chinese translation
- 2-4 common collocations or usage frames
- synonyms and antonyms only when meaning, register, and usage are close enough
- a memory aid only when it is honest and likely to help

## Teaching Notes

Distinguish senses instead of blending them. Mark countability, transitivity,
register, or common prepositions when these are frequent sources of errors. Do
not give an antonym merely to fill a field.

## Visual Pattern

Draw one concrete scene that disambiguates the target sense. Integrate the
headword as a title, not as text inside the generated illustration. For abstract
words, use a simple physical situation rather than an opaque symbol.

## Card Layout

- top: headword, IPA, word class
- left: Chinese and English meanings
- center or right: instructional line illustration
- bottom: example, collocations, and recall cue
