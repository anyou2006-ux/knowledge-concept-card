# High-School Chinese Profile

Use for classical Chinese function words, literary devices, reading methods,
argument structures, ancient poetry techniques, composition methods, and
curriculum-aligned language concepts.

## Required Content

Choose only the relevant fields:

- exact term, source text, author, and context when a quotation is used
- original text with reliable punctuation
- key word annotations, grammar, or rhetorical device
- literal meaning and modern Chinese interpretation
- structural role: how the sentence, paragraph, poem, or argument works
- evidence from the source material
- exam-use cue: how to identify, answer, appreciate, or apply the concept
- one contrast with a common confusing term or error when useful

## Teaching Notes

Do not fabricate quotations, authors, text editions, or historical claims. Keep
appreciation grounded in wording, structure, imagery, and context rather than
empty labels such as 'vivid' or 'profound'.

## Visual Pattern

Use an annotation map, argument-flow diagram, imagery relationship chart, or a
restrained scene sketch that points back to exact language evidence. Treat
original text as typeset text, not generated-image text.

## Card Layout

- top: concept and source or question type
- center: original wording / evidence with callouts
- side: interpretation and structural analysis
- bottom: answering pattern or writing transfer
